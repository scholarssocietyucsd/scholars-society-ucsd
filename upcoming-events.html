Career Opportunity: Healthcare Recruiter at Amergis

Amergis, a healthcare staffing agency, is hiring full-time Healthcare Recruiters in Westchester, Los Angeles. This opportunity is ideal for graduating seniors interested in recruiting, sales, human resources, or talent acquisition.

Compensation: $1,079 weekly base salary plus uncapped commission.

Schedule: Monday remote; Thursday and Friday in person. Additional hybrid scheduling details are available upon inquiry. Hours are 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, with the option for some 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM days.

Benefits: PTO, accrued sick hours, 401(k), medical, dental, and vision insurance.

Responsibilities: Source and interview candidates, manage onboarding and candidate relationships, and maintain a strong candidate pool for healthcare staffing clients.

Preferred qualifications: Experience in sales, recruiting, HR, or talent acquisition, and a college degree in HR, sales, business, or a related field.

Contact Mariana Reyes at mareyess@amergis.com
